Our Products

Platform Capabilities

Built for real-world intelligence

Every GMA-AI product is backed by the same core principles: data accuracy, AI augmentation, and practical usability.

  • Automatic transaction processing — import, deduplicate, and categorize financial data from multiple institutions with zero manual effort.
  • AI-assisted categorization and matching — pattern-based rules with an LLM fallback learn from corrections over time.
  • Subscription billing via Stripe — metered and flat-rate plans, payment methods, invoicing, and webhook processing handled end-to-end.
  • Structured business assessments — Clearscope BDI guides practitioners through repeatable evaluation and outcome measurement workflows.
  • Local-first AI — sensitive financial data stays on your infrastructure; cloud AI is available as an optional augmentation layer, not a requirement.
About

About GMA-AI

GMA-AI is an independent software company founded and led by Bradley Green. We design and build intelligent software tools that give individuals and businesses a clearer picture of their finances and operations — powered by practical AI, not hype.

Our work is grounded in real-world problems: tracking where money goes, identifying patterns in business performance, and removing the manual burden of data work. Each product in the GMA-AI platform is production-tested against real data before it reaches customers.

We are headquartered in the United States and operate as a registered business entity.
